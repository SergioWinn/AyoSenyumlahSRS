# Ayo Senyumlah

Self-authored from the user interview, 16 September 2026.

## Interview

1. Audience and purpose: members of a JKT48 fan community. The app helps them find friends so nobody has to attend the event alone.
2. Vibe: simple, modern, professional, explicitly no AI slop.
3. Journey: open directly on the 2-Shot / Meet & Greet timetable, then offer one clear input action.
4. Energy: calm and task-focused. The community schedule is the main event.
5. Feeling and peak: clarity when a session filter immediately reveals who will be there.
6. Signature move: smooth, friendly interaction that supports the task rather than decorating it.
7. Aesthetic range: premium-minimal.
8. World and assets: distinct functional chapters; no existing brand assets.

## Journey

1. Recognition: the active event and two ticket modes are immediately visible.
2. Orientation: filters narrow the timetable without moving the user away.
3. Connection: occupied lanes reveal familiar community names.
4. Action: one dialog handles manual and CSV input.
5. Resolution: the filtered recap becomes a copyable meeting ribbon.

## Feeling curve

1. Calm: warm paper, compact event context, no marketing hero.
2. Control: filters and tabs respond without page jumps.
3. Belonging: occupied slots carry names, not anonymous counts.
4. Confidence: input validates against the cached master timetable.
5. Readiness: the copied recap is ready to share with the community.

## Peak

“I picked a session and immediately saw exactly who I could meet there.” The peak lives in the timetable filtering state.

## Tell-someone sentence

It is the site where the timetable turns into a list of friends you can actually meet.

## Authored silence

No pinned scroll, cinematic hero, generated imagery, or ambient loops. The timetable earns the screen space.
